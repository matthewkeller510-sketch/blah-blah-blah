## Milestone 3 (docs/MILESTONE-3.md)

The owner asked implementation to continue using best judgement. These are the judgements; none changes a CONFIRMED rule.

| ID | Topic | Decision | Status | Where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D31 | Variable pockets vs persistent placements | A pocket's version follows a deterministic schedule per pocket (`Config.Pockets.cycleSeconds`) so servers converge; a change waits until the pocket is empty of players, survivors (`PocketBlocker`), bags of online owners and ropes of online owners, and is telegraphed. Records placed in a pocket carry its version; when the version no longer exists here they **migrate** (persisted) to the pocket's documented anchor chamber with an honest notice, never deleted. Offline owners' records migrate when next loaded. Chalk and signs do not block a change; ropes do. | PROPOSED (sec 04, 16; resolves the F08/F09 tension) | `PocketService`, `Logic/Pockets`, `NavRecords`, `RecoveryBags` |
